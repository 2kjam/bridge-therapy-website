Your First EMDR Sessions

EMDR does not begin with trauma processing right away. Early sessions focus on:

  • Building trust

  • Learning coping skills

  • Ensuring emotional safety

Your therapist will never rush the process.

During an EMDR Session

A typical EMDR session includes:

  • Brief focus on a memory

  • Bilateral stimulation

  • Noticing thoughts, emotions, or sensations

  • Allowing the brain to naturally shift

You remain fully aware and in control.

Is EMDR Overwhelming?

Most clients are surprised by how manageable EMDR feels. Sessions are paced carefully and always end with grounding and stabilization.
