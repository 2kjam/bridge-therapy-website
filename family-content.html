Questions about getting started

Is child counseling the same as family counseling?

Child counseling focuses on the child’s needs, with caregiver involvement discussed with the counselor. Family counseling focuses on relationships and concerns involving family members. Ask our office which starting point may fit your situation.

What ages do your counselors see?

Age ranges vary by counselor. Tell our office your child’s age and the support you’re seeking so we can discuss an appropriate match and current availability.

Do both parents need to attend?

Ask our office before scheduling who needs to participate and what consent or paperwork is required for your family’s circumstances.

Can we use insurance?

Contact our office about your specific plan, counselor, and type of session. Coverage for an individual child’s appointment and a family session may differ; confirm benefits before your visit.

What if I’m looking for support for myself or my relationship?

Explore Individual Counseling or Marriage & Couples Counseling. You can also contact our office without choosing a service first.
